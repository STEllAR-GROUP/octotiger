# Application validation of SSP history transfer

**Validation snapshot, 2026-10-13.** Direct history transfer is implemented.
All eight SSP CTests and the separate real format-1 compatibility checks pass.
Completed moving-mesh hydro and moving-star runs retain two-stage updates
after the initial RK3 step, and hydro and gravity AMR restart comparisons are
byte-identical. The full moving-star transfer and rebootstrap-baseline
evolutions include actual refinement and coarsening. The controlled smooth-flow
study retains approximately second-order density L1 convergence with errors
within 0.35% of the matched RK3-rebootstrap reference. Maximum-cell errors
converge more slowly in both methods. These results do not establish a general
hydro/gravity positivity guarantee.

See [SSP multistep integration](ssp-multistep.md) for the implemented algorithm
and its assumptions. The [fixed-space temporal](ssp-history-remap-accuracy.md),
[joint space/time](ssp-history-joint-convergence.md), and
[virtual-history](ssp-history-reconstruction.md) experiments are separate
scalar evidence, not substitutes for these application tests.

## Completed regression and compatibility checks

The eight tests `coefficients`, `history_amr`, `hydro`, `restart_hydro`,
`gravity`, `restart_gravity`, `amr_hydro`, and `amr_gravity` passed under the
`test_problems.ssp_multistep` prefix. The final run completed in 462.55 seconds;
its complete log is `output/ssp-amr-validation-20261013/eight-ctests-final.log`.
These cover coefficient algebra, transfer helpers, unchanged default RK3
behavior, fixed-mesh hydro/gravity evolution, exact restart, and variable
timesteps. The two AMR tests additionally require accepted updates after
refinement and coarsening, plus exact restart through later mesh changes.

Compatibility fixtures were generated by a real format-1 executable, rather
than by changing a version marker in a new checkpoint. Both hydro and gravity
inside-batch fixtures resumed at step 3 for two further steps, with all nine
node archives identical to the corresponding uninterrupted continuation.
Format-1 fixtures at step 5 were rejected before advancing with the specific
regrid-boundary recovery diagnostic. New format-2 checkpoints preserve history
at these boundaries.

Local evidence for this snapshot is in `/tmp/ssp-amr-ctest.log`,
`/tmp/ssp-amr-legacy-hydro.log`, and `/tmp/ssp-amr-legacy-gravity.log`. These are
run records, not portable repository fixtures.

## Moving hydro problem and reference

`AMR_ADVECTION` transports a compact smooth density bump through the actual
three-dimensional AMR hierarchy on `[-1,1]^3`. The velocity is `(1,0,0)`, the
pressure is `0.01`, and gamma is `5/3`. The density is

\[
\rho(x,y,z,t)=1+0.2\,b((x+0.4-t)/0.25)\,b(y/0.25)\,b(z/0.25),
\qquad b(s)=\max(1-s^2,0)^4.
\]

Initialization and the external validation reference use exact cell averages
from the polynomial primitive. The final error is volume-weighted over the
current leaf cells, including spatial and remapping errors. Density `L1` is
normalized by the domain volume, 8. The comparison uses the existing spatial
solver, CFL 0.4, refinement threshold 1.005, one species, gravity off, zero
rotation, one HPX worker, and CPU `LEGACY` kernels. The bump moves from `x=-0.4`
to `x=0.4` by the final time 0.8.

The `transfer` executable keeps history across regrids. The matching
`baseline` executable retains the earlier RK3 bootstrap at every scheduled
batch/regrid boundary; it is not an all-RK3 control. Each executable chooses
its own permitted timesteps, so accepted-step counts can differ. Actual
refinement and coarsening are identified from saved node/leaf inventories,
not inferred solely from node counts.

## Completed contact-steepening-on results

These runs use the production default `cdisc_detect=on`.

| Minimum:maximum level | Method | Final density L1 | Accepted steps | RK3 steps | Total RHS evaluations |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0:2 | Transfer | 5.110577e-5 | 50 | 1 | 101 |
| 0:2 | Rebootstrap baseline | 5.099749e-5 | 45 | 9 | 99 |
| 1:3 | Transfer | 4.696519e-5 | 100 | 1 | 201 |
| 1:3 | Rebootstrap baseline | 4.707136e-5 | 90 | 18 | 198 |

The RHS totals are the sums of the reported stage counts. These runs establish
the absence of repeated RK3 startup in the transfer path, not a reduction in
total RHS work or a wall-clock speedup.

In the level-0:2 transfer run, refinement occurs at step 5 and coarsening at
step 50. Restarting from the post-refinement step-5 checkpoint reproduces all
41 final node archives, totaling 14,383,456 bytes, and the global metadata
exactly. All 45 resumed steps use two stages. Coarsening occurs at the final
step in this case: it tests the final coarsened archive, but not a subsequent
accepted update on that coarsened mesh.

The level-1:3 transfer run refines at steps 15 and 80 and coarsens at steps 25
and 90, then reaches step 100. It therefore advances after both kinds of mesh
change. The matching baseline refines at steps 15 and 70 and coarsens at steps
25 and 80. Exact restart of this full time-0.8 level-1:3 transfer case was not performed.
The shorter time-0.35 regression below checks restart through the early
refinement and coarsening events separately.

The two methods have closely matching errors, but the apparent density L1
orders between these resolutions are only 0.1219 and 0.1156. Contact steepening
activates on this initially smooth density profile, so these two results do
not establish the expected smooth convergence order. The shared low observed
order must not be attributed to history transfer alone. Independent checks
find unit x velocity and constant pressure to roundoff: the largest pressure
error across these four final states is below `6.8e-16`, with transverse
velocities below `2.5e-15`. All inspected densities and conservative thermal
energies are positive.

The stored hydro summaries are
`/tmp/ssp-amr-app-validation-20261013/results.json` and
`/tmp/ssp-amr-app-finer-20261013/results.json`. The latter is a partial study:
only the completed level-1:3 pair is reported here. Pressure/velocity checks
are recorded in `/tmp/ssp-amr-solution-analysis.json`.

### Hydro conservation from mesh integrals and outflow ledgers

The initial summaries' printed zero diagnostic drifts are superseded by a
separate check that integrates the saved leaf fields and adds timestamp-matched
boundary outflow ledgers. Maximum drifts below are absolute changes over saved
snapshots, normalized by `max(1, abs(initial integral))`.

| Transfer run | Final time | Maximum mass-plus-outflow drift | Maximum energy-plus-outflow drift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Levels 0:2, extended run | 0.9 | 6.659942e-16 | 8.621336e-16 |
| Levels 1:3 | 0.8 | 1.331988e-15 | 3.879601e-15 |

The extended coarse run is distinct from the time-0.8 accuracy pair above. Its
mesh mass decreases by `2.04429913e-8`, balanced by a boundary outflow of
`2.04429904e-8`; mesh energy decreases by `1.02214948e-8`, balanced by an outflow
of `1.02214948e-8`. Checking mesh integrals alone would incorrectly report a
conservation loss. Across saved snapshots, the unnormalized mass/energy ledger
drifts are at most `5.33e-15`/`3.56e-15` for the coarse run and
`1.07e-14`/`1.60e-14` for the middle run. This establishes roundoff-scale ledger
conservation for these sampled hydro runs, not an arbitrary-state theorem.
The detailed check is `/tmp/ssp-amr-precise-conservation-review.json`.

## Completed controlled smooth-convergence study

This separate study disables contact steepening with `--contact-steepening off`
and refines space and CFL-limited time together. All six runs reach time 0.8.
`h_min` is 1/16, 1/32, and 1/64 at maximum levels 2, 3, and 4 respectively.

| Levels | Method | Density L1 | L1 order | Final density Linf | Maximum saved density Linf |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0:2 | Transfer | 8.054685e-5 | — | 8.178750e-2 | 8.178750e-2 |
| 0:2 | Rebootstrap | 8.060006e-5 | — | 8.182493e-2 | 8.182493e-2 |
| 1:3 | Transfer | 2.109503e-5 | 1.932925 | 4.097258e-2 | 4.097258e-2 |
| 1:3 | Rebootstrap | 2.106578e-5 | 1.935880 | 4.095243e-2 | 4.113250e-2 |
| 2:4 | Transfer | 5.246023e-6 | 2.007607 | 1.660786e-2 | 1.660786e-2 |
| 2:4 | Rebootstrap | 5.228238e-6 | 2.010505 | 1.658505e-2 | 1.658505e-2 |

The maximum L1 over saved snapshots equals the final L1 for each run, so its
observed orders are also those in the table. Saved snapshots occur every five
accepted steps and at the final state; these maxima do not sample every stage.
Final Linf orders are 0.9972 and 1.3028 for transfer, versus 0.9986 and 1.3041
for rebootstrap. Thus this study establishes comparable overall errors and
approximately second-order L1 convergence, not second-order Linf convergence
or fixed-space third-order temporal accuracy.

Transfer and reference L1 errors differ by 0.066%, 0.139%, and 0.340% at the
three resolutions; final Linf errors differ by at most 0.138%. The middle and
finest transfer runs advance after both refinement and coarsening. The coarse
pair refines four parents at step 5 but does not coarsen before time 0.8;
`--allow-no-topology-change` permits that case in the resolution study.
It does not waive the stricter topology requirements of the regressions.

| Transfer levels | Refinement steps | Coarsening steps | Accepted steps | RK3 steps |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| 0:2 | 5 | None | 50 | 1 |
| 1:3 | 15, 75 | 25, 85 | 100 | 1 |
| 2:4 | 40, 105, 165 | 35, 100, 160 | 200 | 1 |

The corresponding reference runs use 45/90/175 accepted steps and 9/18/35 RK3
steps. The total RHS counts are 101/201/401 for transfer and 99/198/385 for
rebootstrap. Removing startup does not reduce total RHS work in this CFL-driven
experiment because the two methods select different admissible timesteps.
The reference also performs the new transfer operations before its forced
startup, so it is unsuitable for a wall-clock speed comparison with the old
implementation.

All six runs maintain positive density and conservative thermal energy in
saved states (minimum density within 2e-14 of 1, minimum thermal energy within
2e-15 of 0.015). Full-precision mass-plus-outflow and energy-plus-outflow ledgers
drift by at most 4.0e-15 and 6.9e-15 respectively under the normalization above.
The preserved complete result is
`output/ssp-amr-validation-20261013/controlled-smooth.json`.

Reproduce the six-run study with:

```sh
python3 test_problems/ssp_multistep/amr_accuracy.py \
  --executable build/octotiger --baseline-executable /path/to/rebootstrap/octotiger \
  --modes transfer baseline --levels 0:2 1:3 2:4 --stop-time 0.8 \
  --contact-steepening off --allow-no-topology-change --skip-restart \
  --output-dir /tmp/ssp-amr-smooth-convergence
```

The harness supports explicit settings and writes commands, binary hashes,
node inventories, errors, and restart results to its output directory. For
example, the strict moving-hydro regression configuration is:

```sh
python3 test_problems/ssp_multistep/amr_accuracy.py \
  --executable build/octotiger --modes transfer --levels 1:3 \
  --stop-time 0.35 --contact-steepening on --require-post-change-steps \
  --output-dir /tmp/ssp-amr-hydro-regression
```

This regression passed with 45 accepted steps and one initial RK3 step. Four
parents refine at step 15, four coarsen at step 25, and evolution continues
after each change. Restarting from step 15 reproduces all 137 final node
archives (48,061,792 bytes) and all four checked master metadata arrays exactly.
All 30 resumed steps use two stages. The preserved result is
`output/ssp-amr-validation-20261013/ctest-amr-hydro.json`.

## Completed moving-star hydro/gravity and exact restart

The `MOVING_STAR` suite tests coupled gravity, conservative bookkeeping,
actual mesh changes, admissibility, and exact restart; it is not an analytic
convergence study. The completed transfer and rebootstrap-baseline runs use
levels 1:2, initial center `(-0.3,-0.3,-0.3)`, velocity `(1,1,1)`, CFL 0.4,
refinement threshold 0.05, moderate atmosphere
`star_rho_out=star_egas_out=0.01`, and `hard_dt=0.005`. Both reach code time 0.8
in 160 accepted steps. A separate all-RK3 control completed only a 15-step
pilot; it is not presented as a full control run.

| Quantity | Transfer | Rebootstrap baseline |
|---|---:|---:|
| RK3 steps | 1 | 32 |
| Multistep steps | 159 | 128 |
| Total RHS evaluations | 321 | 352 |
| Minimum density | 4.024694e-3 | 4.026125e-3 |
| Minimum entropy variable | 1.691142e-2 | 1.690578e-2 |
| Minimum EOS pressure | 5.291845e-4 | 5.289550e-4 |
| Minimum conservative thermal energy | -8.918384e-3 | -8.939469e-3 |
| Maximum absolute total-energy ledger drift | 6.287392e-10 | 9.980086e-10 |

Both runs refine three parents at step 20 and one at step 35, then coarsen one
parent at step 85, three at step 100, and three at step 150. They advance after
both refinement and coarsening and finish with 17 nodes and 15 leaves. The
transfer run uses two stages for every step after initial startup.

Density, entropy, and EOS pressure remain positive in every saved snapshot;
species densities are nonnegative. Conservative thermal energy becomes
negative in a small region in **both** methods, with a maximum of ten cells
and volume `0.00244140625` in the domain of volume 8. The existing dual-energy
path supplies positive pressure there. This result must not be described as
unconditional conservative thermal-energy positivity.

The full-precision mesh integrals plus timestamp-matched outflow ledgers pass
the mass/species tolerance of `1e-10`. Maximum mass drift divided by initial
total mass is `3.585503e-15` for transfer and `3.452707e-15` for the baseline;
the corresponding species drifts are at most `2.523133e-15` and `2.788725e-15`.
Total energy includes half the potential energy and cumulative boundary
outflow. Its maximum relative drift is `5.577016e-9` for transfer and
`8.852494e-9` for the baseline. The smaller transfer drift is a measured
comparison, not an analytic energy-accuracy criterion or a gravity convergence
result.

These completed runs are recorded in
`/tmp/ssp-amr-gravity-validated-20261013/results.json`. Restart from the
post-refinement step-20 checkpoint at code time 0.1 reproduces all 17 final
node archives, totaling 6,995,674 bytes, plus the checked master metadata
exactly. The 140 resumed steps all use two stages and reproduce the original
stage/ratio sequence through the later step-35 refinement and step-85,
step-100, and step-150 coarsening events. The application gravity/restart
suite has therefore completed successfully for this configuration.

The original cold-atmosphere trial with `hard_dt=0.01` failed its first entropy
update in both the old and new RK3 executables, before multistep history was
used. That trial does not identify an SSP history-transfer regression. The
moderate atmosphere is an explicit test change, not a claim that the cold
configuration has been repaired.

## Registered coverage and remaining scope

With NumPy available, CMake registers `test_problems.ssp_multistep.amr_hydro`
and, when interaction lists are enabled, `test_problems.ssp_multistep.amr_gravity`.
Both passed in the final eight-test run. The moving-hydro test requires
refinement and coarsening followed by accepted steps, one initial RK3 step,
conservation checks, and exact restart through a later topology change. The
moving-star harness checks the corresponding coupled-gravity behavior. The
new tests' CTest invocations passed, including the independently repeated
gravity evolution and exact restart. The preserved results and run commands
are in `output/ssp-amr-validation-20261013/`.

All recorded application results are from one locality with CPU kernels.
Multilocality execution, GPU backends, arbitrary equations of state, shocks,
cold stellar surfaces, and a general SSP/positivity proof are outside the
completed coverage. The smooth advection study validates the tested joint
space/time L1 behavior; it does not remove the fixed-space temporal defect identified by the scalar
experiment or establish accuracy for the untested regimes above.
